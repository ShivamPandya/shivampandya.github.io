What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

By Shivam Pandya  ·  Arctique Consulting

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring content, evidence, and entity signals so large language models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini) synthesize and cite your brand when answering complex prompts.

Researchers introduced the term in late 2023 to evaluate how source visibility shifts in AI-synthesized responses. Their findings showed that adding authoritative citations, statistics, and unambiguous entity markers dramatically raised model inclusion.

Questions

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in lists of search results. AEO shapes your content so search features and assistants can lift a direct answer from it. GEO aims to get your brand cited or named inside answers written by generative AI systems. All three rest on the same base: clear, trustworthy, well-structured content.

Can you guarantee my brand will appear in AI answers?

No. These systems change often and no one outside them controls what they say. I improve the things you do control, which are clarity, structure, evidence and consistency, and then measure what appears over time.

How long does it take to see results?

Technical and content fixes can be live within a few weeks. Changes in how often you are cited usually take longer, often a few months, because systems need time to recrawl and re-evaluate your pages.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your site and analytics, a list of your main services, and the questions customers ask before they buy. Competitor names help too.

Do you also do creative work?

Yes. Positioning, messaging and visual direction are part of the work, because answer engines can only repeat ideas that are clear and distinctive.
