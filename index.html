Questions

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in lists of search results. AEO shapes your content so search features and assistants can lift a direct answer from it. GEO aims to get your brand cited or named inside answers written by generative AI systems. All three rest on the same base: clear, trustworthy, well-structured content.

Can you guarantee my brand will appear in AI answers?

No. These systems change often and no one outside them controls what they say. I improve the things you do control, which are clarity, structure, evidence and consistency, and then measure what appears over time.

How long does it take to see results?

Technical and content fixes can be live within a few weeks. Changes in how often you are cited usually take longer, often a few months, because systems need time to recrawl and re-evaluate your pages.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your site and analytics, a list of your main services, and the questions customers ask before they buy. Competitor names help too.

Do you also do creative work?

Yes. Positioning, messaging and visual direction are part of the work, because answer engines can only repeat ideas that are clear and distinctive.

Definition  ·  6 min read

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

By Shivam Pandya, founder of Arctique Consulting  ·  Published Oct 20, 2026  ·  Updated Oct 20, 2026

Short answer

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your content and brand easy for AI systems to find, understand and cite when they write answers. The goal is to be named in the answer, not only listed in the results.

Where the term comes from

Researchers used the name “generative engine optimization” in a 2023 academic paper that studied how to raise a source’s visibility in AI-written answers. In their tests, adding relevant statistics, quotations and citations to content raised how prominently it appeared.

The idea has since spread through marketing. It sits next to two older terms: search engine optimization (SEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO).

GEO, AEO and SEO

The three overlap, but each aims at a different outcome.

SEOAEOGEO
GoalRank pages in lists of resultsBe the direct answer in snippets, voice and AI summariesBe cited or recommended in text written by AI systems
Measured byRankings, impressions, clicksAnswer features won, branded queriesMentions and citations in AI answers
Helps mostRelevance, links, technical healthConcise answers, structured data, question-led headingsClear facts about who you are, evidence, consistent mentions on trusted sites

What tends to help

  • Answer the question in the first two sentences of a page, then explain.
  • Say who you are and what you do in plain words, and keep that description identical across your site and profiles.
  • Add original evidence: data, examples and named sources.
  • Use structured data that matches what is visible on the page.
  • Keep pages crawlable and fast, and check that your robots.txt does not block the crawlers of systems you want to cite you.
  • Earn mentions on sites your buyers and these systems already trust.

What no one can promise

No one can guarantee a place in an AI answer. The systems differ, change often and do not publish their ranking rules. Good GEO improves the inputs you control and then measures the outcome on a regular schedule.

Quick questions

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. Most AI systems still rely on the open web, and the same foundations, such as clear content, good structure and trusted sources, support both.

Do I need new content for GEO?

Often you need better versions of existing pages first: clearer definitions, direct answers and evidence. New notes then fill the questions your site does not yet answer.
